Learning Designed for Excellence

Quality education from the foundation stage to senior secondary level, equipping every child with the knowledge, skills, values and confidence needed for a glorious future.

Classes and Programmes

Divine Heritage Academy offers a complete programme from early childhood to Senior Secondary School.

Early Years

Playgroup, Pre-Nursery, Nursery 1 to 3

The foundation stage, where children begin their education in a safe and caring setting that builds knowledge, skills, values and confidence.

Primary Education

Primary 1 to 5

A continuing programme that develops each child academically, morally, socially and creatively.

Junior Secondary School

JSS 1 to 3

Students are prepared for and registered for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE), also known as Junior WAEC.

Senior Secondary School

SSS 1 to 3

Students are prepared for and registered for WAEC and NECO, with JAMB registration and guidance on tertiary admission.

Examinations

Our students are prepared for and registered for the major national examinations:

  • BECE (Basic Education Certificate Examination), also known as Junior WAEC
  • WAEC (West African Senior School Certificate Examination)
  • NECO (National Examinations Council)

Mission

To provide quality education in a safe and supportive environment, while nurturing students academically, morally, socially, and creatively, and preparing them for a glorious future.
