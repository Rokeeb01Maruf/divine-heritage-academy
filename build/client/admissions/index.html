Admissions

Divine Heritage Academy welcomes students into all classes, subject to availability of space.

Admission Requirements

Please prepare the following when applying:

  • 1Completed admission/application form.
  • 2Recent passport photograph.
  • 3Copy of the child’s birth certificate or age declaration.
  • 4Previous school report card/result, where applicable.
  • 5Transfer/withdrawal certificate for students transferring from another school.
  • 6Relevant examination results for students seeking admission into JSS or SS classes.
  • 7Placement assessment/interview where required.
  • 8Payment of the applicable admission and school fees.

School Fees

Fees are structured according to the student’s class and level of study. Parents and guardians receive a detailed breakdown of fees at the point of admission.

For current admission fees and a complete breakdown of charges, please contact the school administration.

Fees may include

  • Tuition fees
  • Registration/admission fee
  • Examination fees
  • Books and learning materials
  • Uniforms
  • Other approved school charges
